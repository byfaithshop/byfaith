Our story

Created through faith

by faith began with a simple prayer: to create beautiful things through my faith, for His glory.

The name comes from Hebrews 11, where ordinary people stepped forward “by faith” without seeing the whole path. The mountain in our logo represents the holy hill, the place of God’s presence and worship, emphasizing drawing near to God and living faithfully before Him.
