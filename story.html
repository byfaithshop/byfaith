The Hebrews 11 story

By faith, they walked.

Our name comes from one of the most beloved chapters in Scripture, a chapter about people who trusted God before they could see the way.

“Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.”
Hebrews 11:1

Hebrews 11 is sometimes called the “Hall of Faith.” Again and again, the same two words open each story: by faith. Not by sight, not by strength, not by certainty. By faith.

These were ordinary people with doubts, families and long waits. What set them apart was simple: they believed God, and they took the next step.

The Hall of Faith

Those who went before us

  1. Hebrews 11:4

    Abel

    “By faith Abel offered unto God a more excellent sacrifice.”

    His worship came from a heart that trusted God, not from routine.

  2. Hebrews 11:5

    Enoch

    “By faith Enoch was translated that he should not see death.”

    He walked so closely with God that he was known as one who pleased Him.

  3. Hebrews 11:7

    Noah

    “By faith Noah prepared an ark to the saving of his house.”

    He built for a flood no one had seen, because God had spoken.

  4. Hebrews 11:8

    Abraham

    “By faith Abraham went out, not knowing whither he went.”

    He left everything familiar and followed God one step at a time.

  5. Hebrews 11:11

    Sarah

    “By faith Sarah judged him faithful who had promised.”

    Long past the age for children, she trusted the One who made the promise.

  6. Hebrews 11:27

    Moses

    “By faith Moses endured, as seeing him who is invisible.”

    He chose God’s people over Egypt’s palaces and led them toward freedom.

  7. Hebrews 11:31

    Rahab

    “By faith Rahab received the spies with peace.”

    An outsider whose courage and faith placed her in the family line of Jesus.

“Wherefore seeing we also are compassed about with so great a cloud of witnesses… let us run with patience the race that is set before us.”
Hebrews 12:1
Meet the founder

Meet Faith

Faith is the founder of by faith, and everything she creates, she creates through walking step by step with Jesus, keeping her eyes on Him. by faith is her way of putting God’s Word into people’s hands: on a card for a friend, a verse tucked into a Bible, or a shirt worn on an ordinary Tuesday.

The mountain in the by faith logo is a reminder of Abraham, who went out “not knowing whither he went.” Faith doesn’t always see the whole path either. But she believes the One who called her is faithful, so she keeps walking.

Her prayer is that each piece brings encouragement to you and to the people you love.
